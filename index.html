— AI DRAFTING & AUTOMATION

The AI reads the register.
Your team reads the draft.

SmartCosec's assistant works only from the entity's own facts and your firm's precedents. It never invents a director or a date — every field links back to the register entry it came from.

Drafted resolutions

Describe the transaction in a sentence — and get the resolution, transfer form and updated register, ready for review.

KYC extraction

Passports, proof of address and constitutions are read on upload. Names, dates and addresses land in the right fields with a confidence score.

Deadline detection

Change a financial year end or file a late return and every dependent obligation recalculates, with the late-fee tier shown before it applies.

Pre-filing review

Filing packs are checked against the register for mismatched names, missing signatures and stale addresses before anything is lodged.

— QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Still have questions? We're here to help.

Contact our team →
Can we migrate from BGL CAS 360 or Viewpoint?
−
Yes. Professional and Firm plans include a guided migration: we import entities, officers, members and document history from your existing export, reconcile registers against the registry record, and flag any differences before go-live.
Does the AI send client data to third-party models?
+
Drafting runs inside SmartCosec's private environment. Client data is never used to train external models, and every AI-populated field remains traceable to its exact source register entry.
Which registries can you lodge with directly?
+
Singapore's BizFile+ is supported for direct electronic submission. For Hong Kong, the UK, Australia, BVI, Malaysia, and Cayman we produce complete, pre-checked filing packs for you or your agent to lodge.
How is SmartCosec related to Smart Audit?
+
Both products share the same underlying platform infrastructure. Firms using both share unified client records, allowing auditors to pull live registers and signed resolutions without back-and-forth emails.
Do clients need their own login?
+
Directors and shareholders receive a white-labeled portal login where they sign, upload documents, and view their compliance calendar. Client portal logins are included across all plans at no extra charge.
