How It Works

STEP 1

Initial Consultation

Understand proposed business activity, structure, and incorporation requirements. Provide advisory on setup approach and compliance considerations.

Duration: Same day (1–3 hours)
STEP 2

AML & KYC Verification

Conduct Anti-Money Laundering (AML) screening and Know-Your-Client (KYC) verification for directors, shareholders, and beneficial owners.

Duration: Same day
STEP 3

Name Application

Submit proposed company name for approval via Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority system, including naming advisory.

Duration: Same day (subject to the name availability)
STEP 4

Document Preparation

Prepare incorporation documents including company constitution, share structure, and officer details.

Duration: Same day
STEP 5

Client Review & Approval

Review all incorporation details with client and obtain final confirmation before submission.

Duration: Same day
STEP 6

Registration Submission

Submit incorporation application electronically to ACRA for processing.

Duration: Instant submission
STEP 7

UEN Issuance

Upon approval, company is officially incorporated and Unique Entity Number (UEN) is issued.

Duration: 1–2 hours (standard)
STEP 8

Post-Incorporation Setup

Provide business profile, statutory registers, and guidance for banking and compliance setup.

Duration: Same day (2–4 hours)
