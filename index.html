Workflow

One annual cycle, run the same way for every entity.

1

Onboard

Import from the registry or a spreadsheet. KYC, controllers and constitution captured once.

  • Registry lookup by UEN / CRN / ACN
  • Bulk import for a book of clients
2

Maintain

Every change — a new director, a transfer of shares — is a register event that generates its own paperwork.

  • Resolution + form + register entry in one action
  • Effective-dated history
3

Resolve & sign

Drafts go to directors through the portal. Signatures return, documents file themselves.

  • Written resolutions or meeting minutes
  • Audit trail per signature
4

File & close

Lodge with the registry, record the acknowledgement, and roll the obligation forward to next year.

  • BizFile+ submission
  • Filing packs for HK, UK, AU, BVI
AI drafting & automation

The AI reads the register. Your team reads the draft.

SmartCosec's assistant works only from the entity's own facts and your firm's precedents. It never invents a director or a date — every populated field links back to the register entry it came from.

AI

Drafted resolutions

Describe the transaction in a sentence — "transfer 10,000 ordinary shares from Tan to Lim at par" — and get the resolution, the transfer form and the updated register, ready for review.

OCR

KYC extraction

Passports, proof of address and constitutions are read on upload. Names, dates and addresses land in the right fields with a confidence score.

DET

Deadline detection

Change a financial year end or file a late return and every dependent obligation recalculates, with the late-fee tier shown before it applies.

CHK

Pre-filing review

Filing packs are checked against the register for mismatched names, missing signatures and stale addresses before anything is lodged.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can we migrate from BGL CAS 360 or Viewpoint?

Yes. Professional and Firm plans include a guided migration: we import entities, officers, members and document history from your existing export, reconcile registers against the registry record, and flag any differences before go-live.

Does the AI send client data to third-party models?

Drafting runs inside SmartCosec's own environment. Client data is not used to train any model, and every AI-populated field is traceable to the register entry it was drawn from.

Which registries can you lodge with directly?

Singapore's BizFile+ is supported for direct submission. For Hong Kong, the UK, Australia, BVI, Malaysia and Cayman we produce complete, pre-checked filing packs for you or your registered agent to lodge.

How is SmartCosec related to Smart Audit?

Both products come from the same team. Firms using both share one client record, so an auditor can pull the current register and signed resolutions without asking the cosec team to send them.

Do clients need their own login?

Directors and shareholders receive a portal login branded to your firm, where they sign, upload and see their own compliance calendar. It is included on every plan and does not count toward your user limit.
