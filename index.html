Key information about setting up and running a compliant Singapore company.

Once your company name is verified and identity documents (passports, proof of address) are cleared, incorporation with ACRA typically completes within 1 to 2 hours on Singapore business days.
Yes! Singapore allows 100% foreign shareholding across almost all business sectors. Under the Singapore Companies Act, every company must appoint at least one locally resident director (a Singapore Citizen, Permanent Resident, or EntrePass / Employment Pass holder).
Every Singapore company must appoint a qualified Corporate Secretary within 6 months of incorporation. The secretary ensures your company complies with the Singapore Companies Act, maintains statutory registers, files Annual Returns with ACRA, and records all board resolutions.
Every Singapore company must: (1) Prepare financial statements, (2) Hold an Annual General Meeting (AGM) within 6 months of its Financial Year End (unless exempted), (3) File an Annual Return (AR) with ACRA within 7 months, and (4) File Corporate Tax (Form C/C-S) with IRAS by November 30.
